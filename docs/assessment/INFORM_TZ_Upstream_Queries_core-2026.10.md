# INFORM Tanzania: queries on the INFORM Sub-national SADC 2024 workbook (release core-2026.10, 2026-10-10)

To: the INFORM team at the Joint Research Centre (JRC) and the SADC Secretariat (DRR Unit). From: the Prime Minister's Office, Disaster Management Department (PMO-DMD), INFORM Tanzania.

INFORM Tanzania reproduces the *Tanzania - Country Model Template.xlsx* (INFORM Sub-national SADC 2024, December 2024 v1.0) exactly, leaf by leaf, as its Core. An indicator-by-indicator recheck of every value against documented events, the other indicators of the workbook and each indicator's own definition found the values below. None has been changed: the live model withholds the ones marked *withheld* (treated as missing until corrected, as the INFORM guidance treats data that fails validation) and shows the note beside every other. We ask the partners to confirm or correct each in the next release of the workbook, and we offer the Tanzanian institution named as the source of a replacement value.
