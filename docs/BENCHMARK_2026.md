# Benchmark 2026: the institutional dataset behind the Core

Release `bm-2026.10`, values dated 2026-10-10. Built by `python3 scripts/build-benchmark.py` from `data-source/benchmark_2026/` (each input produced by a script in `scripts/benchmark/` from the public source named below) into `src/data/tanzania-benchmark-2026.json`, which the engine applies as approved institutional values (`src/engine/risk/benchmark.ts`, `model.ts`).

Benchmark 2026: institutional values prepared from public, citable datasets for the indicators the SADC workbook leaves empty or carries with values that failed validation. Applied by the engine as approved institutional values (lineage shown), supersedable through the data portal; the workbook itself is unchanged.

## What the benchmark does

- The workbook (INFORM Sub-national SADC 2024) is never changed: `buildModel({}, { workbookOnly: true })` still builds it exactly, and the parity tests prove it.
- A benchmark value is an approved institutional value of its date. It shows its lineage wherever it is displayed, it lifts the quarantine of the workbook value it replaces, and an institution supersedes it through the data portal (the more recent approved value wins).
- Template indicators the workbook lists without data are activated with a Tanzanian definition (unit, keyed level, direction, normalisation and reference range), as the workbook's Metadata sheet does for every indicator in use. The reference range of an activated indicator is resolved from the Tanzanian data, the workbook's own rule for its 'Data range' indicators.
- The data portal's tools carry the activated indicators as ordinary columns (`scripts/build-collection-tool.py`), and `inform_sheets/INFORM_TZ_Collection_Benchmark_2026.xlsx` is the tool with the benchmark values filled in (green cells), the version institutions continue from.

### Methods

- **HA.NAT.CH-ERO** (coastal-erosion): scripts/benchmark/coastal_exposure.py + build-benchmark.py: 1 km hotspot points (certainty good) joined to the nearest council within 1.5 km; the council value is the point-count-weighted mean of rate_time over segments retreating significantly (rate < 0, p < 0.05); 0 where no segment retreats; inland councils (more than 500 m from the Indian Ocean polygon) 0. Replaces the workbook's coastal erosion values (0 for every unit, data note DN-05) with the same source correctly joined to the councils.
- **HA.NAT.CH-SEA** (coastal-lecz): scripts/benchmark/coastal_exposure.py: GLO-90 tiles mosaicked; cells with elevation 0-10 m inside the council and within 25 km of the Indian Ocean polygon, divided by the council's land cells; inland councils 0. Activates the template's sea-level-rise indicator with the standard exposure measure (the low-elevation coastal zone of CIESIN/McGranahan et al. 2007).
- **HA.NAT.ST-TC2** (cyclone-swath): scripts/benchmark/cyclone_swaths.py: for every track point with sustained wind of 34 kt or more, a circle of the reported 34-kt wind radius (largest quadrant; 100 km when not reported); the union of the circles of all storms intersected with the council polygon. Replaces the workbook's cyclone exposure (one non-zero unit, Mtwara 18.1%) with an observed-track exposure for every council; the workbook's reference range (0 to 18.1%) is kept, so every council with at least 18.1% of its area inside a swath scores 10.
- **HA.NAT.HW-EXP** (heatwave-days): scripts/benchmark/gee_tmax_daily.py (council-mean daily Tmax) + heatwave_days.py (15-day-window percentile, 3-day runs). Activates the template's heatwave indicator with the WMO/ETCCDI-style warm-spell definition, which is relative to each place's own climate so that the humid coast and islands register as the rest of the country does.
- **HA.NAT.LI-CAS** (lightning-density): scripts/benchmark/lightning_zonal.py: annual stroke density summed over the monthly climatology, mean of the 5-arc-minute cells touching the council. Activates the template's lightning indicator (listed as casualties, for which no register exists) with the physical hazard density; the Lake Victoria basin and the western and southern highlands show the highest densities.
- **HA.HUM.ACC** (road-crashes): scripts/benchmark/road_crashes.py: each police region's recorded crashes (mean of 2024 and 2025) shared among its councils in proportion to their 2022 census population, divided by the council's land area (scripts/benchmark/land_area.py). Activates the template's vehicle-accidents indicator with where crashes happen: the density of crashes is highest in the big cities (Zanzibar Town, the Dar es Salaam municipalities, Moshi, Mbeya, Mwanza, Arusha), where most road-traffic injuries occur (65.7% of 7,092 injured patients in Sawe et al. 2021, Emergency Medicine International, doi:10.1155/2021/4272781). Police records count serious crashes; until 2019 they also counted injury and damage-only crashes, of which Dar es Salaam recorded more than half (NBS 2018, 2019).
- **HA.HUM.HMAT** (hazmat-sites): scripts/benchmark/osm_hazmat.py (OpenStreetMap installations), nemc_certificates.py (the NEMC monthly certificate lists read, placed in their council from the ward and classified) and hazmat_sites.py: mapped points closer than 100 m are one site; certificates added by council; sites divided by the council's land area. Activates the template's hazardous-material indicator. The cities and their industrial corridors lead (Dar es Salaam, Zanzibar Town, Moshi, Mwanza and Ilemela, Arusha, Tanga, Mbeya, Morogoro, Kibaha and Mkuranga), followed by the gold-mining councils, where the NEMC lists certify carbon-in-pulp plants and tailings facilities. NEMC's Lake Victoria roadmap for Mwanza City (2023) counts 51 industries there and names the Nyashishi river as the carrier of industrial effluent into the lake. A site counts once whatever its size.
- **VU.SE.LV-FS** (ipc-phase2plus): build-benchmark.py from ipc_2026_districts.csv: 100 minus the Phase 1 share; districts matched to councils by the registry's district. Replaces the workbook's food-security values (0 in 127 councils, 149% in one, data note DN-06) for the 30 analysed districts; the reference range is re-resolved to 0-100%.
- **VU.SE.LV-IPC** (ipc-area-phase): build-benchmark.py: the report's area phase for the 30 analysed districts (Crisis where 20% or more of the population is in Phase 3 or worse); Phase 1 elsewhere. Activates the template's IPC classification indicator on the IPC's own 1-5 scale.
- **CC.INF.COM-ELEC** (census-electricity): scripts/benchmark/census_profiles.py: grid (TANESCO) + solar + generator shares of households by council; where a profile gives only the grid share by council (Pwani, Ruvuma) that share is used. Replaces the workbook's regional Global Data Lab values (Dodoma 100%, data note DN-08) with the census by council.
- **CC.INF.COM-PHONE** (census-phone): scripts/benchmark/census_profiles.py: 'any mobile phone, both sexes' by council. Replaces the workbook's regional values with the census by council (persons rather than households).
- **CC.INF.EDU-ALIT** (census-literacy): scripts/benchmark/census_profiles.py: 2022 census, both sexes, by council. Replaces the workbook's national literacy value with the census by council.
- **VU.VG.ECO-FEMHH** (census-female-headed): scripts/benchmark/census_profiles.py: 2022 share of households headed by a woman, by council. Activates the template's female-headed-households indicator with the census.
- **VU.VG.HC-DISAB** (census-disability): scripts/benchmark/census_profiles.py: prevalence, both sexes, by council. Activates the template's disability indicator with the census.
- **HA.NAT.LS-EXP** (landslide-ukerewe): build-benchmark.py from landslide_wb_ranks.csv: the island has no valid hazard cell in the global map (every cell is water/no-data, which the workbook counted as fully exposed); the share of medium-to-high cells is 0. Replaces the workbook's Ukerewe value (1.0, the national maximum, data note DN-04); the global map's own ranks reproduce the workbook for the other councils (r = 0.77 with the share of rank 3-4 cells).
- **CC.INS.DRR-ARR** (drr-arrangements): build-benchmark.py from drr_status.csv: a council has its district's plan and assessment, its region's team, centre and training; an arrangement not listed is not in place (the registers are PMO-DMD's own); the share of the five, as src/engine/risk/institutional.ts scores an approved committee record. Adds a Tanzanian indicator to the workbook's DRR implementation group beside its two national ones (Sendai score, anticipatory-action coverage), so that a council's own preparedness lowers its lack of coping capacity and, through the council means, its region's and the country's (decision of 10 October 2026). The national value is high while the registers are incomplete and falls as PMO-DMD records more districts.
- **CC.INS.GOV-DMC** (committee-functioning): build-benchmark.py from drr_status.csv: a criterion recorded 1 or 0 is assessed; the share of the assessed criteria met; a council with no assessed criterion has no value (the group keeps the workbook's two indicators), as src/engine/risk/institutional.ts scores an approved committee record. Adds a Tanzanian indicator to the workbook's Governance group beside its national government-effectiveness score and regional corruption index. The legal framework (the Disaster Management Act 2022 and the policy) is the same everywhere and is not scored; the differences PMO-DMD sees are in collaboration and involvement.
- **VU.VG.DP-IDP** (idmc-idp): build-benchmark.py: 23 displacement updates with a region or district in their location; an update naming several regions is split equally; the national stock of 75,117 is allocated in proportion to each region's share of the 2023-2025 displacements; regions without a recorded displacement 0. Replaces the workbook's displacement values (the national stock of 30,080 written on Kaskazini Unguja alone, Manyara 0 after the Hanang landslide; data note DN-07) with a documented regional allocation of IDMC's own figures.

## Re-resolved reference ranges

- `VU.SE.LV-FS`: 0 to 100. The workbook resolved this percentage's range from its own data (0 to 149); a share of population runs from 0 to 100.

## What remains without a dataset

- Zoonoses, plants and pests (HA.NAT.ZPP-AD, ZPP-PD, ZPP-PE): no validated national dataset at council level yet; the group stays empty and is excluded from the hazard mean, as INFORM treats missing data. Candidate sources are listed in the register (issue DATA-05).
- Internal violence (HA.HUM.VIO-EVE, VIO-FAT): withheld from the live model by programme decision (data note DN-12); the workbook keeps its ACLED counts.
- DRR training of the committees and the committee criteria are recorded for a few regions and councils so far: the registers of PMO-DMD and the committees' records through the Data portal complete them.
- Agricultural drought frequency for the five Dar es Salaam councils stays withheld (not applicable to a city; data note DN-01).
